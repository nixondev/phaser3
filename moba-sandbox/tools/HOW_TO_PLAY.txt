MOBA SANDBOX — a roguelite built on MOBA laning
================================================

Run "MOBA Sandbox.exe". Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because
the file isn't signed: click "More info" -> "Run anyway".

THE RUN
  Pick a hero. A run is endless rounds on one lane: destroy the enemy
  tower to win a round; lose your tower and the run is over. Your best
  round is saved.

  Towers are SHIELDED against heroes: you can only damage one while at
  least one of your creeps is inside its range circle (a leashed creep
  counts). Bring a wave. In overtime the shields drop.

  Gold comes from last hitting: only your KILLING BLOW on an enemy creep
  pays (the white tick on their health bars marks your attack damage).
  Dying costs some of your gold, and the enemy hero takes it if it got the
  kill.

  From overtime (a few minutes in, later each round) kills pay no gold,
  tower shields drop, towers take more and more damage, and waves grow:
  finish the round. Every hero death in overtime sends a siege minion with
  the other side's next wave: it can't be stopped and blows up on the tower
  for 10% of its health.

  Between rounds, spend gold in the shop: switch heroes, level your
  Q/W/E/R, buy upgrades and creep (wave) upgrades. Every pick makes prices
  rise a little. The enemy shops too, and gets smarter as the run goes on.

CONTROLS
  Right-click        move (hold to keep steering)
  Left-click         attack the enemy nearest the cursor
  Left-click         on your own creep below half health: deny it (nobody
                     gets its gold)
  Q W E R            cast at the cursor the moment you press (quick cast)
                     (some abilities have a second press: SHOCK, POUNCE)
                     Prefer to aim first? Esc menu -> Cast indicator ON:
                     hold to see the aim, release to cast, right-click cancels
  T                  with the cursor on one of your creeps: leash it — it
                     follows you and attacks what you attack (T on it again
                     to release; with Cast indicator ON: T, then click)
  Hover a Q W E R    see what the ability does right now
  Click your hero    (top left) fold / unfold your upgrade list; the
  box                enemy's panel (top right) folds the same way
  S                  stop
  Mouse to an edge   pan the camera (or arrow keys, or click the minimap)
  Space              jump the camera to your hero; Y locks it on
  Esc                pause (resume, abandon run, quit)
  F11                fullscreen / window
  F9                 in Training: back to hero select

TRAINING
  From the hero-pick screen's Training mode button (not from inside a run).
  Dummies to practise on, plus an enemy tower to the south-east with creep
  waves for both sides every 25 s (last hits, denies, leashing, the tower
  shield). Top right: No cooldowns and No mana switches, and a free Shop
  (or press B) to try any build. 1-4 switches hero.

YOUR BASE
  Standing in your own base (the well) heals you, and nothing can hurt you
  there. An enemy hero standing in it burns.
